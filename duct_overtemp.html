DUCT OVERTEMP Annunciator

Duct Over-Temperature Warning - Air Conditioning System Alert

Caution Description
Annunciator
DUCT OVERTEMP
Condition
Duct temperature has reached approximately 300°F due to low airflow in the ducting system
Trigger Point
Duct over-temperature sensor detects temperature ≥ 300°F, illuminating amber DUCT OVERTEMP caution light
Air Conditioning System Overview
Bleed Air Heating System

Bleed air from the compressor of each engine is delivered into the cabin for heating and pressurization. When airborne, bleed air is mixed with outside ambient air from the ambient air valve in each flow control unit.

The air mixes with recirculated cabin air and is routed aft through the floor-outlet duct, which handles 75% of the total airflow.

Temperature Control

The automatic temperature control modulates bypass valves and air conditioning compressor:

  • For greater heating: Bleed air bypasses the air-to-air heat exchangers
  • For greater cooling: Bleed air passes through air-to-air heat exchangers
Airflow Distribution

Approximately 75% of recirculated air passes through floor outlets while 25% is routed through ceiling outlets.

Pressurized air discharged from floor outlets is always warmer than air from ceiling outlets when bleed air valves are OPEN.

Control Locations
ENVIRONMENTAL Control Section

Located on the copilot's left subpanel, contains:

  • BLEED AIR VALVE switches
  • VENT BLOWER control switch (HI-LO-AUTO)
  • CABIN TEMP MODE selector switch
  • MANUAL TEMP switch (INCREASE/DECREASE)
  • CABIN TEMP level control
Air Control Knobs

Located on subpanels for cockpit comfort regulation:

  • PILOT AIR CONTROL KNOB
  • DEFROST AIR CONTROL KNOB
  • CABIN AIR CONTROL KNOB
  • COPILOT AIR CONTROL KNOB

When fully pulled out: Maximum airflow to cockpit

When fully pushed in: Minimum airflow to cockpit

System Operation Notes
Bypass Valve Operation

In manual mode, the MANUAL TEMP switch modulates bypass valves in the bleed-air lines:

  • Allow one minute (30 seconds per valve) for both valves to move fully open or closed
  • Only one valve moves at a time
  • Varies amount of bleed air routed through air-to-air heat exchanger
NOTE: The air-conditioner compressor does not operate unless the bypass valves are closed. To ensure valves are closed, select MAN COOL then hold MANUAL TEMP switch in DECR position for one minute.
Vent Blower Operation

VENT BLOWER switch has three positions:

  • AUTO: Blower operates at low speed if CABIN TEMP MODE selector is not OFF
  • LO: Blower operates at low speed regardless of mode selector
  • HI: Blower operates at high speed regardless of mode selector
Warnings and Notes
Important Considerations
WARNING: DUCT OVERTEMP indicates potentially dangerous temperature conditions in the air distribution system. Immediate action is required to prevent system damage or fire risk.
NOTE: During high-altitude flights, cool-night flights, and flights in cold weather, the ceiling outlets should be closed for maximum cabin heating efficiency.
